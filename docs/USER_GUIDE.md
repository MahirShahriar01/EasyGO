# Customer Guide

## Getting started
1. Open the site and click **Register** (top right). Enter your name, e-mail and a password with at least 8 characters including letters and numbers. You're signed in immediately and get a welcome notification with code **WELCOME10**.
2. Already registered? Click **Sign in**. Tick *Keep me signed in* to stay signed in for 30 days.
3. Forgot your password? Use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page — a reset link is e-mailed to you.
4. Toggle **dark mode** with the moon/sun icon in the header.

## Searching
Use the search box on the home page and pick a tab:

| Tab | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Hotels | Destination or hotel name, check-in/out dates, adults, children, rooms |
| Flights | From, To (type a city — suggestions appear; ⇄ swaps them), date, travellers and class |
| Buses | From, To, journey date |
| Tours | Destination or tour name, travel date |
| Cars | Pick-up city, pick-up and drop-off dates |

On the results page you can refine with the **filters** on the left (tap *Show filters* on mobile) and change the **sort order** at the top. The URL updates as you filter, so you can bookmark or share your search.

## Hotels
* Hotel cards show stars, guest rating (out of 10), and the lowest nightly price. Cards marked *Sold out for your dates* have no free rooms.
* On a hotel page: browse the **photo gallery** (click to enlarge, use ← → keys), read amenities and policies, see the map and **guest reviews**.
* In *Availability*, adjust dates/guests and press **Update**; each room shows its live availability, bed type, size, breakfast and cancellation policy. Choose the number of rooms and press **Reserve**.

## Flights
Results show airline, times, duration, stops, baggage, refundability and seats left. Filter by stops, airline, fare type and price; press **Select** to continue.

## Buses — choosing seats
Click **Select seats** on a trip. On the seat map, grey seats are taken, white are free, green are your selection (max 6). The summary on the right shows the total; press **Continue to checkout**.

## Tours and cars
Pick a departure date and number of travellers (tours) or pick-up/drop-off dates and number of cars (cars), then press **Book this tour** / **Reserve car**.

## Checkout
1. **Your details** — check the contact name, e-mail and mobile number (the confirmation goes to this e-mail). For flights and buses enter each traveller's name as on their ID (passport optional for flights). Add special requests if any.
2. **Coupon** — type a code in the price panel and press **Apply**. The discount appears in the price breakdown, or an explanation if the code can't be used.
3. Accept the terms and press **Continue to payment**. Your booking is now **held for 30 minutes**.

## Payment
A timer shows how long the reservation is held. Choose:
* **Card** — number, name, expiry, CVC.
* **bKash / Nagad / Rocket** — enter your 11-digit account number, press **Send OTP**, then enter the 6-digit code.
* **Pay at property** (hotels only) — your room is confirmed now and paid at check-in.

After a successful payment you see the confirmation page with your **booking reference** (e.g. `EG-7K3QX9PD`) and receive an e-mail.

> Demo installation: any 16-digit card works (ending `0002` = declined); any wallet number with any 6-digit OTP except `000000`.

## Managing bookings (My account)
* **Dashboard** — counts and upcoming trips.
* **My bookings** — filter by Upcoming / Past / Cancelled and by service. Open a booking to see the full **e-ticket / voucher**.
* **Print / PDF** — prints a clean ticket (use *Save as PDF* in the print dialog).
* **Pay now** — finish paying a pending booking.
* **Cancel booking** — shows how much will be refunded before you confirm:

| Service | Refund |
|---|---|
| Hotel | 100 % for refundable rooms cancelled ≥ 24 h before check-in, otherwise 0 % |
| Flight | 90 % for refundable fares, non-refundable fares 0 % |
| Bus | 100 % ≥ 24 h before departure, otherwise 50 % |
| Tour | 100 % ≥ 7 days before, 50 % ≥ 2 days before, otherwise 0 % |
| Car | 100 % ≥ 24 h before pick-up, otherwise 0 % |

* **Reviews** — after a confirmed or completed booking a review form appears on the booking page. Reviews appear publicly after moderation.
* **Wishlist** — tap ♥ on any hotel, tour or car to save it.
* **Notifications** — the bell shows booking confirmations, cancellations and other alerts.
* **Profile & security** — update your details and photo, or change your password (other devices are signed out).

## Ads
Some pages show clearly-labelled *Sponsored* content. Banners can be closed with ×; full-screen ads show *You can skip in N s* and can be closed once the countdown ends (or close themselves automatically).
